The Japanese Joint Health Discovery

In a small Japanese village called Yuzurihara, many seniors in their 70s, 80s and even 90s continue farming daily while maintaining remarkable mobility and flexibility.

Researchers discovered their diet contains foods naturally rich in hyaluronan — a molecule that helps keep synovial fluid thick, lubricated and supportive for healthy joints.

As we age, healthy hyaluronan levels naturally decline, which may affect joint lubrication, flexibility and comfort over time.
